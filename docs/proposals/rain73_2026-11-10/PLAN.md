# rain73 — dynamic rain, storms and fog for the attic window, skylight and town

The user asked: *"Why don't they move? Make it more realistic. I want it dynamic: sometimes light rain, other times insane storms
that cover the view with fog."* They also asked for fog rolling in around the town.

**Branches:**
- `rain73`, off `polish73`, in root, mod and engine (`polish73-eng` → `rain73-eng`).
- Stage 0 (this plan) is written.
- The polish73 hotfixes for the same area are already on polish73, so they can ship first:
  - the rain sheet clipped out of the wing;
  - tileable skylight drops;
  - the seamless cloud deck plus a scud layer;
  - the LOOK UP framing;
  - the ambience mix and the HIGH level (exe).

## Where it lives today

- The weather director is `WxUpdate` in `code/client/cl_uiscene.cpp` (menu-attic67).
  - It picks fair / overcast / rain / storm as fixed states (55 / 22 / 16 / 7 %), held 20–40 minutes.
  - It eases amounts over about 20 s and drives `scenewx` roles: sky, cloud, tint, lights, rain, flash, plane.
- Rain on screen is all static content from `gen_window_view.py`:
  - two scrolling streak sheets, `hzm_wx_rain_far` and `hzm_wx_rain_near`;
  - one static clampmap of drops on the gable pane, `hzm_wx_drops`;
  - a scrolled tile on the skylight, `hzm_wx_skydrops`.
- Sound comes from six looping beds plus one-shots, in `WxAmbience`.

## Design

**1. A continuous intensity instead of fixed states.**
- `WxUpdate` keeps the clock and the fair / overcast logic, but rain becomes a continuous intensity I in 0..1.
- Bands: drizzle < 0.15, light < 0.35, steady < 0.6, heavy < 0.85, storm ≥ 0.85.
- I follows a slow random walk shaped into showers. Each shower builds over 1–3 minutes, peaks, then eases off.
- Frequency matches today's mix (mostly fair, regular rain, an occasional big storm). A storm peak is held 1–4 minutes.
- Fog is a separate scalar F:
  - calm morning fog (dawn and morning, no storm, about as often as storms), rolling in over about a minute, holding, then lifting;
  - storm haze, where F follows I above 0.75.
- Never fog plus a thunderstorm at once, except for the storm's own haze.
- Exported for content and debugging: `ui_wxRain`, `ui_wxFog` (read-only mirrors), and `ui_wxForce "I F"` for QA.

**2. Drops on the glass.** This is engine-side and gated to the menu scene.
- A small CPU drop system per pane: the gable and the skylight, each with its own 2D frame and gravity along its slope.
- Drops appear at a rate set by I, grow, merge when they touch, and run down once over a size threshold.
- Running drops leave a fading wet trail.
- Each drop is drawn as a textured quad (a lens sprite: dark top, light bottom, a rim) into the scene, after the opaque pass.
- About 300 drops per pane at storm peak. That's well under a millisecond, and nothing allocates per frame.
- No flipbook, so nothing can visibly loop.

**3. Falling rain outside.**
- An engine particle field of short streak sprites in a box outside each opening: the gable and the skylight.
- Streaks are spread across three depth layers, with density from I and slant from the wind (wind follows I and gusts).
- They're lit by the lightning flash.
- Because they're spawned in boxes clipped to the openings' outside half-spaces, they can never enter the room.
- This retires the two streak sheets.

**4. Fog.**
- 3–5 soft fog-bank cards in the town, at the ridges, the valley and the low ground, drifting slowly. They're tinted by the daylight and glow faintly under lit windows at night.
- Each card's alpha comes from F and its distance: far ridges and the spire fade to silhouettes, then vanish; near roofs only soften.
- They use the existing `tint` / `lights` roles, plus a new `fog` role (alpha = F × card depth weight).
- At storm peak, F ≈ 0.9 swallows everything past the nearest roofs, and the flash lights the fog cards.
- The cards are generated by `wx_town.py` / `gen_window_view.py`, sit outside the gable plane only, and add no load time (a few small textures).

**5. Sound follows I.**
- Window patter, roof drumming and the outside wash each scale with I.
- Wind gusts follow I.
- Thunder is scheduled from the flash it belongs to: the delay is the strike's distance, and loudness follows the delay.
- Fog damps the far one-shots by about −6 dB with a low-pass `_h` swap, mutes the planes and the loudspeaker, and keeps the church bell.
