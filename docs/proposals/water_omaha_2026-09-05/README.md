# Ocean / shoreline / underwater on Omaha - research only (2026-09-05)

Three lanes, nothing built. Read in this order: `LANE-A_current_state_measured.md` (what the water IS today, measured from the BSP and the engine), `renderer_capabilities.md` (what gl1/gl2 can do, with a scorecard of earlier claims), `WATER_MODERN_TECHNIQUES.md` (each modern technique, its minimum renderer feature, and a gain/risk ranking). The two rankings agree on the cheap wins: wet-sand swash and shore-locked foam on `mohtest/omaha_set4_shoreline` (7 free stages, phase-locked to the existing flap for free), splash against obstacles (shipped 2026-09-05 as `coop_obstacleWash`), `deepbluesea` translucent from below (one missing `blendFunc`), caustics on the seabed terrain, and the ~30-line gl2 `alphaGen dot` port that also fixes nine shipped foliage stages. Gerstner/FFT waves, SSR and refraction are not reachable without engine work.
