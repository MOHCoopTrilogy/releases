## Bugs found (the tips stay true; logged separately)
- **bug-3416**: the AT Team and Grenadier script guns (`coop_at_rocket_loop`, `coop_grenade_lob_loop`; also the Battalion's trooper 1) spawn inside their carrier's bounding box and have no team filter. In the run, the grenadier was **killed by his own scripted grenade**, the AT loader took 67 hp from the AT gun, and the gunner was at 37/150 hp 4 s after spawning. The native AI already fires both weapons, so the loops are redundant. Not fixed.
- The same run also showed prone-shooter guns still hitting Germans for 12 hp a round. This is evidence for the open bug-3360, not a new entry.
