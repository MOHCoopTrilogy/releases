HZM Coop Mod · design for review · 14 October 2026

The officer and his reinforcements

A proposal for making the enemy officer and every wave he calls behave like a German platoon and its leader, including the MG42 team that was scrapped.

Design only. Nothing is built, nothing in the mod has changed.

What the officer does today

On most combat maps a high-health officer appears at a fixed spot with a radio and one to four bodyguards. He stands and shoots like any soldier. When he first sees a player, when his health passes three quarters, half and a third, and each time a bodyguard dies, he calls a wave. The wave is picked by a dice roll out of eight kinds. It appears at one fixed point per map and every man in it runs at the nearest player until he is close, then fights on his own.

He heals himself twice when the players lose sight of him. Killing him ends the calls. Blowing up the radio stops them for good.

What is weak about it

  • The dice choose the wave. An anti-tank team comes for players on foot. A dive bomber is as likely indoors as in the open (it is then swapped for a rifle squad).
  • One doorway per map. Every wave appears at the same point. Nothing checks whether a player is looking at it, standing near it, or whether a path leads from it to the players.
  • No squads, only men. Every soldier runs the same loop: go to the nearest player, stop close, shoot. No leader, no covering fire, no reaction to losses, no retreat.
  • The kinds differ by weapon only. Elite squad and Wehrmacht squad behave the same. The grenade men run to the front rank. The sniper is alone, walks toward the players and never moves again. The dogs arrive apart from their handler.
  • The officer does not lead. He has no place behind his men and gives no orders beyond the radio call.
  • His death changes little. Unless a switch that ships turned off is on, the survivors carry on as before.
  • The MG42 team is dead code. It was replaced by the Wehrmacht squad because the gunner floated and never fired.

The officer, as proposed

He commands from behind

He takes real cover a short way behind his escort, out of the players' line of fire. Two escorts stand forward, the rest stay at his side.

He gives the order

A whistle blast, then the radio call. Both sounds are already in the game.

He reads the fight

Players bunched in the open get the bomber, the guns or the MG. Players in a building get the assault group and grenades. A player on his own gets the dogs. Players holding at long range get snipers. No kind twice in a row.

He moves when threatened

A player close with a clear shot, or a hard hit, and he falls back to the next cover toward his radio while the escort covers him.

Without his escort he runs

He stops fighting and heads for the radio to call more men. Catch him on the way and the call never happens.

Kill him and the attack breaks

Squads that are already at half strength give up, using the surrender system the mod has. The rest pull back to where they came from and hold there.

Unchanged: his health scaling, his two heals, the radio objective, the replacement officer, the music, XP and challenges.

The reinforcements, one by one

The eight kinds stay eight, with the same names in the feed, the same per-map switches and the same challenge. What changes is who is in them, where they come from and how they fight. Every squad on foot gets a leader, moves in two halves (one shoots while the other moves to cover), and stops at the distance its job needs.

The piece that was never rolled out

The MG42 team

The old version put a finished gun nest at the spawn point. The gunner floated, and he never fired because the script that drives every working nest in the game was never started for it. The proposal does not repair the nest. It builds a team, using what was made for the paratroopers' .30 cal.

CarryThe gunner brings the gun in with the rifle section. An assistant carries ammunition and takes over if he dies.
Choose groundA wall top or window sill he can rest the bipod on, overlooking the players, or flat open ground. Never a doorway. The walk there is checked first.
SuppressBursts, with pauses before the barrel overheats. A belt and one spare. It fires so the other half of the squad can move.
RelocateWhen a player gets on its flank, when a grenade lands near it, or when it has no target, the team packs up and sets up somewhere else.
CaptureKill both men and the gun stays there with what is left on its belt. Any player can use it.

It is part of the rifle section, about half the time and only on open ground. Not indoors. A per-map switch can turn it off. This is the largest single step of the work.

How encounters vary

  • Where they come from. For each wave the game looks at the map's own spawn points and a ring of spots around the players, keeps those no player can see, that are not close to a player, and from which a walkable path leads to the players, and draws one. The next wave prefers a different side.
  • What comes. The officer's choice follows what the players are doing, so two runs of a map differ when the players play differently.
  • One call held back. He keeps a reserve and spends it on a player who wanders off, a dug-in squad, or his own MG42 turned against him.
  • A roll per session decides flank side, sniper spot and whether the rifle section brings the MG.

Player count

The numbers stay with the scaling the mod already has. Squad sizes are written for two players. A solo player meets smaller, weaker squads. Three and four players meet bigger ones, up to six men a squad. Health and accuracy follow the same curve as today. The MG team and the spotter come out of the squad's headcount. The only addition is the sniper's spotter (two men where there was one).

What it will not do

  • Spawn where a player is looking, or close to a player.
  • Spawn on ground with no path to the players.
  • Stand on or block objective items, scripted doors or ladders.
  • Add models or textures. No new download for anyone.
  • Touch the Omaha maps, or any map where the officer is turned off.
  • Change the eight kinds, their feed lines, the per-map switches or the challenges.
  • Stay on if you do not want it. One setting brings back today's behaviour exactly.

Build order

Each step is built apart from the game files, tested on two maps at solo and scaled sizes, shown in stills and a GIF, and only then put in.
