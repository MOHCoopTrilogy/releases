	- Restored ladder sliding. Hold crouch to descend faster.

		^ Cannot kick a grenade if out of sight (behind a solid wall) or grenade is moving too fast.
	- Restored player's scream after falling for a long time.

	- Restored Allied friendly fire and kill confirm responses (Base game).
	- Restored Allied reloading lines (Expansions).
	- (Partially) Fixed NPC's behaviour with Expansion weapons.
	- Fixed NPCs cutting off their grenade throw sentence with grenade spotted sentence.
	- Reduced NPCs accuracy while blind firing.
	- Adjusted starting ammo for all maps.
	- Rebalanced weapons. Highlights:
		- Pistols: Increased fire rate and accuracy.
		- Hi-Standard: Damage reduced, firerate increased a bit.
		- Beretta: Magazine size decreased from 15 to 8, decreased accuracy and bash damage.
		- DeLisle: Damage reduced.
		- KAR98 Sniper: Rate of fire increased to match KAR98 (singleplayer only).
		- G43, SVT (Spearhead): Recoil reduced, rate of fire increased.
		- PPSH: Fire rate and spread increased. Magazine size increased to 70 in Spearhead.
			^ Higher fire rate impossible because of required server FPS.
		- Shotgun: Accuracy and range greatly increased. Projectile count decreased, but damage increased to compensate for it.
	- Bomba A Mano now explodes on impact and can't be cooked.
		^ You can still hold attack and throw the bomb farther, or become the impact bomb.
	- Player now gets lower difficulty medals if game is completed on a higher difficulty.
