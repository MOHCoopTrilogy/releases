# Skin credits

The HZM Coop Mod ships community-made Allied player skins. None of them carry an explicit
licence grant - the 2002-2003 MOHAA scene released these to be used, with credit in a readme as
the convention. We honour that convention here: every author below is credited, with the page
the skin came from. If you are an author and want your work removed, say so and it comes out.

Sources: the AAAA MOHAA archive (mohaaaa.co.uk) and ModDB.

### Packs
- **1st Rangers** (Rookie One, 2003) - ModDB. 6 skins.
- **BA_skin_pack1** - community compilation; 12 Allied skins (Band of Brothers officers, British
  regulars/paras, Polish para, snow Brits, recon captain).

### Modifications made
- Four shader names in BA_skin_pack1 collided with ours (`bob45holster`, `lt_helmet`,
  `pvt_helmet`, `sgt_helmet`); THEIR copies were renamed `ba_*` and their tiks retargeted so
  neither pack's appearance changes. No art was altered.
- No textures or models were edited. `zzz-Ranger_Medic` ("please do not edit") ships unmodified.

## Weapon variant / import authorship (skins-hunt research, 2026-08-19)

Resolved by archived-page + local-readme evidence (full report: _research/skins_hunt_2026-08-19.md):

- **MOHPA ports (pagarand, PA colt; PA sniper on GB)** - **acme313** (archived FileFront page,
  Developer field; his colt readme self-credits). The PA BAR port is ambiguous (vdog77 also
  ported one, explicitly claiming no credit) - left at pack-level credit.
- **WW1 arsenal (P14, G98, MP18, C96 trench, M1911 WWI, BAR M1918, M1903, SMLE)** - weapons by
  **East** (Medal of Honor World War 1 mod; skins Magna_Carta, MP18 anims from SH assets).
  "WW1 Extended" (MOHI-KAN) contributed fixes/menus only, no weapons. NOTE East's readme:
  contents not to be used without permission (Spearhead-derived material + Dark'nStein anims
  excepted) - CONTACT EAST BEFORE ANY PUBLIC RELEASE ANNOUNCEMENT of these (ModDB fallback;
  MATSEAST92@msn.com is 2009-era).
- **Thompson M1A1 remodel** - model DaRKaNGeL, skins Apocalypse. **Strapped StG44** - DarkAngel.
- **Thompson 1928 drum** - model Dr.Deleto, reskin/anim swap Static.
- **Thompson 27A1 commando** - FlakRider (strap texture by Hobbs).
- **M1911 Bloody EIC** - Schutze (EIC series). **BAR M1918 classic/A1/A2 trio** - stlkid.
- **DP-28** - Lt. Pato (Patricio Mardones) - readme grants reuse WITH CREDIT (the only explicit
  yes in the pile). **Johnson M1941 / Mauser C96 / S&W M10** - East (no-alteration readmes;
  displayed credit honors the spirit; modifications technically need his OK).
- **FG42** (scoped and irons) - **DaRKaNGeL**: the re-rigged FG42 skeleton and its animations come from his 2005 animated FG42 add-on (moh-db.com/mods/17867), on EA's original FG42 mesh. Since v1.10.66 the FG 42 Irons draws a new CC BY mesh on that skeleton (see Weapon models (CC) below); the scoped FG42 still uses EA's mesh. (Corrected 2026-11-08: this line wrongly said in-house; a retroactive permission request to him is pending.)
- **Kar98 rifle-grenade (Gewehrgranate)** - stock EA SH/BT asset.
- Still pending author: tommy28 (M1928), MP40 Reactivated, M1911 Covert, 1911 Classic (drbond
  key suggests "Dr. Bond"?), Authentic-wood Winchester.

Models under a Creative Commons licence, credited as the licence asks (author, title, link, licence, changes). Each also ships a credits file inside its pak.

## Finish set 2 (in progress, docs/proposals/finishes2_2026-11-09)

- **Camo / wear / engraving tiles, insignia and plates, US awards and badges** - original artwork drawn by script for
  the mod (tools/patterns.py, decals.py, medals.py). The insignia and decorations reproduce US Army / Free French /
  Afrika Korps designs, which are public domain (US government insignia; the DAK palm is drawn without the swastika).
- **Nose art** (tools/noseart2.py, list with URLs in assets/noseart_sources.tsv) - recreated from public-domain WWII
  photographs on Wikimedia Commons and repainted in a period palette with brush texture, chips and fading:
  - *Shark mouth* - "A Chinese soldier guards a line of American P-40 fighter planes, painted with the shark-face emblem
    of the Flying Tigers", US National Archives (NARA 535531), c.1942, public domain (US government work).
    https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:A_Chinese_soldier_guards_a_line_of_American_P-40_fighter_planes,_painted_with_the_shark-face_emblem_of_the_Flying..._-_NARA_-_535531.jpg
  - *LIBERTY BELLE* lettering - "41-23920 Liberty Belle" (B-24D), San Diego Air & Space Museum archives, public domain
    on Commons (no known copyright restrictions). The painted figure in that photo is not used.
    https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:41-23920_Liberty_Belle.jpg
  - *Mission tally* - original period-style drawing.
- Insignia lettering (82nd AA, WaA, souvenir, AIRBORNE tab, VALOR) is rendered with Windows system fonts into the
  images (Arial Bold, Courier New Bold); no font files ship. The nose art uses no fonts.
