## v1.10.92 - Anchor & Anvil (fixes)

Your disc missions are converted again automatically to pick these up.

### Anchor & Anvil
- **Enemies are there from the start.** The dock workers and their opening conversation, and every group, now appear when the original has them, instead of popping in late or not at all. Patrols walk their routes.
- **The fire escape by the U-boat pens** can be climbed standing.
- **You can shoot over railings and low walls.**
- **Sharper textures:** quay concrete, warehouse brick, walls, streets and rooftops use new high-quality textures. Signs, plates and crate lettering stay readable.
- The weapons case lid is lit, and enemies scale with the number of players, like Sealed Orders.

### Sealed Orders
- **The jeep is back** at the start, with its crew and the mine ambush.
- **Lighter smoke** from the mine and crate breaks, as in the original.
- **Every enemy the original has is there:** 27 missing soldiers restored, the far-bank wave always comes, and the ambushers wait for their moment.
- The bridge hold keeps you respawning at the bridge.

### Their Dying Breath
- **The church defence can't stall** when players die during it: you come back at the church.

### Also
- Fixed a dog animation error.
